---
title: "unrequited?"
date: "2026-06-12"
---

I have built cathedrals from the silences between your sentences,
lit them with the almost glances you never meant.
Inside, the air is incense and almost~
beautiful enough to kneel for,
heavy enough to break the knees.

I do not wish for the sun to move
I only watch the way it paints your shoulder~
when you turn to speak to someone else,
tracing the quiet geometry of a space
I am allowed to see, but never crowd.

I never wanted your love.
Not in the same way, people write songs about it~
all storms and confessions,
all hands reaching across impossible directions
I only wanted to keep finding in ordinary places..

In the pause before you reply.
In a photograph taken by someone else.
In the way your name arrived 
and rearranged an entire afternoon.
I keep you like a rare book on a high shelf~
admired, intact and entirely unopened
